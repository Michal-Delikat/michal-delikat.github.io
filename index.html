PROCESS

How we work together

A simple, transparent process, so you always know what's happening and what I need from you.

  1. 1

    Conversation & brief

    We talk about your business, your customers and your goals, and look at what your competitors are doing.

  2. 2

    Design

    I prepare a mockup with layout, typography and colours for your approval before building anything.

  3. 3

    Build

    I develop the website, set up the technical SEO and add your content.

  4. 4

    Launch

    Domain, hosting, testing and going live. I make sure everything works on every device.

  5. 5

    Care

    After launch I keep your website updated, secure and improving, as much or as little as you need.
